Privacy

The short version

This site has no accounts, no forms, no ads and no newsletter. The only data collected is anonymous visitor statistics, and only if you agree to it.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read and which store buttons are used. It runs in Google's Consent Mode:

  • With your consent, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) to recognise returning visits.
  • Without your consent, no cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device. Google Analytics only receives anonymous, cookieless signals (such as a page being viewed), which Google uses to estimate total visits. They can't be linked to you or to your other visits.

Advertising features are switched off for everyone: no ads, no ad personalisation, nothing shared for advertising.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (not in a cookie) so the question isn't asked on every page. You can change it at any time: .

The browser demo

The browser demo of Avalon: Legacy Edition asks before it collects anything. Only if you say yes does it send play statistics to GameAnalytics: how far you get in the story, and which site sent you to the demo (taken from utm_ parameters in the link, or else from the referring site). GameAnalytics also records what it records for every game: your device, operating system and browser version, and your sessions and how long they last. There is no name, no account and no ads.

With your consent, the GameAnalytics script keeps a random user id, a session count and any events not yet sent in your browser's local storage (keys starting with GA::). It sets no cookies. If you say no, GameAnalytics is never started and nothing is sent or stored. If you switch it off later, sending stops; what was already stored stays until you clear the site's data in your browser.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage too. You can change it at any time in the demo under OPTIONS → STATISTICS. The demo's Steam and Epic Games Store buttons add utm_ parameters to their links so the stores can see that a visit came from the demo; after that, the store's own privacy policy applies.

Videos

Trailers are hosted on YouTube. Nothing from YouTube loads until you press play; the player then comes from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and Google's privacy policy applies.

Store links

Buttons for Steam, the Epic Games Store, Google Play, the App Store and the Seven Mages Store (merch, run by Spreadshop) are plain links. Once you follow one, you are on that store's website and its privacy policy applies.
