Privacy

The short version

This site has no accounts, no forms, no ads and no newsletter. The only data collected is anonymous visitor statistics, and only if you agree to it.

Analytics

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read and which store buttons are used. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) and IP addresses are anonymised. Without your consent, no Google script is loaded and no cookies are set.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (not in a cookie) so the question isn't asked on every page. You can change it at any time: .

Videos

Trailers are hosted on YouTube. Nothing from YouTube loads until you press play; the player then comes from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and Google's privacy policy applies.

Store links

Buttons for Steam, the Epic Games Store, Google Play and the App Store are plain links. Once you follow one, you are on that store's website and its privacy policy applies.
