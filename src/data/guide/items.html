You can use items in Avalon to regain life or to fight creatures. These items you can use in battles:

Handgrenade
Heavy Grenade
All medic kits (first aid, small, large and 2000)
Paralyser
Light Defpen
Heavy Defpen
Baby Powersucker
Strong PW sucker

Some of the items can also be used outside the battlescreen. These are:

All medic kits (first aid, small, large and 2000)
HIV virus
Nuclear Missile (but I doubt you would want to use it ;)

For more detailed information on where to buy and how to use the items, please take a look at the item list below.
