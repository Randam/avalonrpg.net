At the bottom of this page you can also listen to some Avalon music in mp3 format.
In the game, you will only hear music if you have a wavetable compatible soundcard such as a Soundblaster Live or AWE32. The music format is MTP, for which you can also download a tracker called MiGTracker Pro at the download section. The format MTP is not compatible with any popular other format such as MID or S3M, but you can download a command-line player for it here.

The music below is listed alphabetically, and there are two complete soundtracks available: a 'normal' and an 'alternate'. The 'alternate' was made later as an enhancement to the original game and it allows you to switch between two different soundtracks.
