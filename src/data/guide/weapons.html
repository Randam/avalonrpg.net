Weapons and defense can be bought at the weaponshops in both the human and the alien village. Only the Vaporizer cannot be bought there. You will get this weapon after defeating Esor Amreh.

Weapons provide 'power', if you have more power, you can hit monsters harder during battle. For example if you have the Heavy Axe, you can hit a monster for 80 damage max.

Cost p/p is the amount of money paid per point of power/strength. The lower this value the better the weapon/defense relatively is for its price.
