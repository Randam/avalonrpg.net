The story

How the last of humanity ended up on a distant planet, and why it is not as empty as it seemed.

The Earth as we know it was almost destroyed by its own inhabitants. The air was heavily polluted, the ozone layer gone. All vegetation had died out. Almost a century earlier the effects of their lifestyle had already become clear to them, but humans being humans, they carried on without thinking about the consequences for their children, and slowly the air became more and more unbreathable.

Then, around 2105, came the terrible war. It started almost instantly after the Chinese destroyed the last nuclear warhead. For more than a century there had been peace in large parts of the world. But suddenly, apparently without any cause, every country in the world attacked its neighbours over some insignificant reason. It was even said that God Himself had left the world.

A group of people known as the Avalon cult believed the world was coming to an end. Groups like that were found all the time, of course, but this one had some interesting ideas. Its goal was to escape the dying Earth before it was too late. That meant stealing a spaceship, which wasn't easy, but they succeeded. Their plan was to use newly invented technology that could freeze people for centuries, and flee to a distant planet called Avalon, which they believed had an Earth-like climate.

✦ ✦ ✦

Their plan worked. On the fourth of July, twenty-five people froze themselves aboard the New Apollo, a spacecraft originally built to fly to Pluto. Its equipment was excellent for their purposes, and its atomic engine could carry them across the great distance they had in mind. They turned on the engines, and their journey into the unknown began.

After almost two hundred years of physical death, the ice in their tubes began to melt, and they awoke. They were indeed in orbit around the planet Avalon. But their moment of victory did not last long: six of them had not survived the artificial sleep.

The first thing they did was scan the planet for alien life. Strangely enough, they found nothing but vegetation. Large forests covered the planet's surface, but according to their scanners there was no animal or intelligent life, which was of course very odd. The colonists didn't pay attention to such details. They didn't double-check their scanners, and simply landed on a good spot on the planet's surface.

They began by building a small village. Not expecting any danger, they didn't fortify their settlement. Four years of happiness and prosperity made the colonists believe they had found their way back to paradise. Their hazardous operation was a complete success. The planet was just like Earth: the air was breathable, the sky blue and full of clouds. An Avalon day lasted twenty-two hours and twenty-seven minutes, and the average temperature was twenty-six degrees Celsius. The colonists discovered several plants they could eat, and the chickens they had brought from Earth were excellent for their eggs and meat.

✦ ✦ ✦

One of the colonists, however, a man called Caddman (nobody knew his real name), just didn't believe they were alone on the planet. He was the only one who had fortified his house, and he kept warning the others about the dangers they might encounter. The villagers thought he was crazy and paranoid, and ignored him. They thought his mind had been damaged during the long journey through space. Caddman told them stories about strange and dangerous creatures in the woods, but they didn't believe him. They trusted their equipment and saw no reason for extra caution.

Unfortunately for them, their ignorance was almost fatal. One sad day the village was suddenly attacked by hundreds of strange creatures. They were totally surprised by such a huge number of foes. Only thanks to the courage of Mace, a young man who had been a warrior during the Great War on Earth, could they resist the attack. Mace managed to kill some of the creatures that seemed to be leading it. Nevertheless, the spaceship was destroyed and there were several casualties.

As quickly as they could, they built a wall to protect the village against new attacks. But paralysed with fear, and knowing nothing about military matters, they couldn't build proper fortifications. When a new attack came, they would almost certainly be slaughtered. Caddman was nowhere to be seen, and most of the remaining colonists believed he had fled and left them to die. Mace, however, didn't believe Caddman was mad. He thought Caddman was looking for a way to defeat the monsters.

Lee, the leader of the colonists, was not so convinced, and decided to end the threat himself. He tried to negotiate with the monsters, but they captured him. Mace, who had already shown his courage and skill by saving the village from the first attack, was now the only one capable of finding out what was going on.

He volunteered to search for Lee and to find out what the monsters wanted, to secure the safety of human life on the planet. He had to succeed, or the remaining villagers would die a quick and violent death, without ever knowing who or what had killed them, or why.
