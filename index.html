Return to a classic world

Avalon: Legacy Edition brings the 1998 DOS RPG back to life. Carefully polished for modern systems, it keeps the charm, atmosphere and gameplay of the original: rough edges, old-school challenges and all.

Humanity has fled a destroyed Earth and found refuge on the distant planet Avalon. The survivors build a small village, believing themselves safe. But appearances can be deceiving…

You play as Mace, a reluctant hero who must venture beyond the village, face dangerous creatures and uncover the truth behind the growing threat.

What awaits you

  • A faithful recreation of the award-winning cult DOS RPG from 1998, by its original creators

  • Classic gameplay and atmosphere, refined for modern systems

  • An old-school adventure full of exploration, quests and turn-based battles

  • Numerous references to classic games, movies and books

  • The complete original 1998 DOS game, hidden somewhere inside

  • Plenty of secrets waiting to be discovered

  • About 10 to 15 hours of playtime

  • No 40-page manual, no skill trees: pick up your gear and go

Where it all began

Avalon, 1998

Avalon started life in 1998 as a freeware DOS RPG, made from scratch by two teenage brothers, Jeroen and Bowie Derwort, in Turbo Pascal, with music composed in their own home-made tracker. In 2000 it won the ZDNet Shareware Award in the category Strategy, and it has been downloaded over 200,000 times.

The original is still free to download and runs great in DOSBox.
