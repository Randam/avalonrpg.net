The complete soundtrack

All {{soundtrack.tracks}} tracks on two discs: the 37 original compositions from 1998, including the songs that never made it into the game, and 34 enhanced versions made for the Legacy Edition. Composed by Jeroen and Bowie Derwort.

Want the rest? The complete soundtrack, with every original track and the enhanced versions, is on Steam.

The Legacy Edition soundtrack

The Legacy Edition plays these same compositions, and lets you choose in the options between the original sound and a new, enhanced version.

MoonSound edition

The soundtrack also exists as a version for the MoonSound, the famous OPL4 sound cartridge for MSX computers.
